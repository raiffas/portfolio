last week was my first week of my half batch at the recurse center. it was a whirlwind of meeting new people, trying new things, and being uncomfortable (in the best way). I had a lot of really great conversations about the nature of being a recurser in an active batch and one piece from my batchmate Rishab that stuck with me was our shared tendency to 'lose hope'. Specifically when things get difficult, losing hope feels like giving up and deciding that its not worth pushing through. Having batchmates to go through this experience with means more points of contact to keep you up when it gets tough, when we feel like losing hope. Staying grounded in the face of doubt and uncertainity is a muscle that needs to be exercised and having a group of lovely people to rely on makes that practice so much more attainable. 

I often lose hope before I've even tried. I would like to have more faith in myself. And I would like to rely on others before relying on other tools to pull me out of the pits of despair. At the end of the day its not that deep right? we are all just learning and moving towards the best versions of ourselves. I can remove the pressure to perform and the expectation to be an expert when I should allow myself to enjoy being a beginner.
