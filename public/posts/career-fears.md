Career Fears

I think one of the worst things about fear and anxiety is this habit of filling your brainspace with negative thoughts meant to perpetuate this feeling of doom. 

Every time things get tough or seem bleak, its so easy to submit oneself to those outcomes instead of pushing through. 

Is that not how we find success? Through continued dedication to a goal that is greater than what we can acheive at the time. 

So instead of allowing myself to wallow in the pain and the doubt that maybe I'm not good enough to fight for the software engineering position I want
I will continue making incremental progress towards my goal. 

I think the scariest part of all this is just the amount of time it really take to get good at something and the feeling that I don't have the time to really do that. 

I feel like I should already be there and every minute that I'm not there already is putting more and more behind. 

But that's because I've decided to look at my situation from the perspective of failure. I've already decided that I'm behind and so I am. 

And you know, maybe some part of that is true. I could've spent the past 10 years grinding leetcode, I could've spent the past 2 years in my job being as technical as I could, I could've ... 

But what good is it to stay stuck in the endless map of decisions that I did not choose?

I am here now, working to be a better programmer, grinding to give myself the best opportunity I can, surrounded by peers that I look up to and resources I can utilize to get ahead. Every minute I am here is another minute that I am dedicated to reaching my goal. And eventually, I will get there because that is how things work. 

If you put in the time and energy, you will acheive your goal. But if the goal is lofty, know that the amount of time and energy needed will be great as well.
