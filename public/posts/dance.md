I've been dancing since 2016. On and off, but its been 10 years since I started my dance journey. I wanted to dance so bad as a teen. I would watch dance crews for hours and hours in my bedroom just imagining what that was like. I never actually danced--not on my own, not with a crew, not in a class. And back then it was mostly because I wasn't allowed to dance. But now that I reflect on it, I think I was also afraid to do it. I think I just loved to watch it and wished I could do it, but never let myself learn how to. Then the opportunities finally came. Classes that I wanted to take (that i could afford on my own). And I just did that. And then it turned into something else. It turned into an opportunity to do it for real. And so I did. I auditioned, I trained, I rehearsed, I stressed, I practiced. I sacrificed. All for the love of dance. But somewhere along the way, the fear became beginner than the love. And i tried to ground myself by reminding myself that 'i love to dance' and 'i'm just afraid to be perceived'. which was true, but it never felt right. Did i *love* dance? I'm struggling with this right now. I am in the birthplace of hip hop and street dance. I'm surrounded by an endless amount of opportunies--more than I've ever had in my life. I'm living the dream. But I'm not craving dance the way I did in the past. It makes me question if my dance journey is just over. What do i want now?

I found a piece of myself that I never really paid attention to. Im a dancer because I love **music**.
I don't know what that means for me right now. or how this will translate to my dance journey. but I want to continue chasing music and that love and hopefully the dance will follow. I mean, 10 years of dancing is not nothing. right now i just don't want to lose everything I've built there but i want to let go of the pressure and the performance and i want to find movement that's mine. and expresses what i truly love.
