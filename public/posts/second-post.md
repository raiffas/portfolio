Shouting into the void and setting my intentions for my corner of the internet.

Is it weird to address a non-existent audience when I write? 

Hi ! I'm creating this space to be an outlet and log of my adventures. I just left my stable, cushy, defense job to live my dreams and go live in NYC. My goal for being here (at least right now) is to really immerse myself in the city. I have no expectations, I have no concrete accomlishments I'm trying to hit, I just want to _live_ in new york city. And I think the pressures of social media and the friends that I made through various channels have made it difficult to stay grounded in that. At least for me. Everyone has their opinions (and everyone is entitled to them!) and everyone wants to know what's going on. Understandable, for a lot of people I am doing the impossible. Just dropping everything and starting over in a place that some people only dream of (including me). The gusto of just saying fuck it to stability and safety and deciding to go even without a job (which I couldn't do without the most incredible safety net there has ever been). But, I don't want to life someone else's experience. I don't want to feel like there's something I should do and I have to take advantage of while I'm here. I'm here to live my life and no one elses (which should always be true, but for me is blatantly clear in this phase of life). 

One of my best friends in the world is moving to NYC with their entire community of found family. 

My brain is reeling from that realization.
